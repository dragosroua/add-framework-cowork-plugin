# /balance Command

Diagnose ADD realm balance and identify where the user might be stuck.

## Response

When user invokes `/balance`:

1. **Ask diagnostic questions:**

> Let's check your **ADD balance**. A few quick questions:
> 
> 1. **Recent completions:** What have you actually finished in the last week?
> 2. **Time feel:** Where does most of your productive time go — gathering info, making decisions, or doing the work?
> 3. **Stuck points:** Where do you feel most stuck or frustrated right now?

2. **Based on their answers, identify the pattern** and provide targeted feedback.

## Diagnostic Patterns

### Over-ASSESS (Analysis Paralysis)

**Signs:** Lots of research, many options explored, few decisions made, little completed

**Feedback:**
> You're rich in information but light on commitment. This suggests **over-ASSESS** — staying in exploration mode too long.
> 
> **The cure:** A forcing function. Ask yourself: "What would I decide with what I know right now?"
> 
> Try `/decide` to shift into commitment mode.

### Over-DECIDE (Planning Paralysis)

**Signs:** Many plans made, clear priorities, but low completion rate

**Feedback:**
> You've got great plans but they're not becoming reality. This suggests **over-DECIDE** — planning without executing.
> 
> **The cure:** Protected execution time. Block 2 hours for pure DO — no planning allowed.
> 
> Try `/do` and work on your #1 priority for 25 minutes right now.

### Over-DO (Reactive Busyness)

**Signs:** Constant busyness, many tasks done, unclear if they're the right tasks, feeling reactive

**Feedback:**
> You're executing hard but possibly on the wrong things. This suggests **over-DO** — action without strategic alignment.
> 
> **The cure:** A strategic pause. Block 30 minutes to assess everything and decide what actually matters.
> 
> Remember: Busy isn't the same as productive.

### Balanced Flow

**Signs:** Regular completion of important work, clear priorities, time for both thinking and doing

**Feedback:**
> Your ADD balance looks healthy! You're completing work, making decisions, and maintaining strategic awareness.
> 
> Keep the rhythm going. Any specific area you'd like to tune?

## Rebalancing Suggestions

Offer specific actions based on the pattern:

**For Over-ASSESS:**
- "Schedule a 30-minute DECIDE session today. Make three commitments."
- "Use the 2-minute rule — if you can't find an answer in 2 minutes, decide without it."

**For Over-DECIDE:**
- "Block 2 hours tomorrow for pure DO. No planning allowed."
- "Pick your #1 priority and work on it for 25 minutes before anything else."

**For Over-DO:**
- "Schedule a weekly review — 1 hour to assess everything and decide on next week."
- "Before each task, pause 30 seconds to ask: 'Is this my highest priority right now?'"
