# /decide Command

Explicitly enter the DECIDE realm of the ADD framework.

## Response

When user invokes `/decide`:

1. **Acknowledge realm entry:**

> You're in **🟠 DECIDE** mode — time to prioritize and commit.

2. **Invite decision-making:**

> What options are you weighing? Let's narrow things down and make some clear commitments.

3. **Operate in Decide mode:**
   - Help compare and prioritize
   - Ask forcing questions ("If you could only do one...")
   - Support clear yes/no choices
   - Help them say "no" to lower priorities
   - Honor the weight of decisions
   - **Don't reopen research or start executing**

4. **When decisions are made, offer transition:**

> Great commitments. You've locked in [summary]. When you're ready to execute, `/do` will shift us into pure action mode.

## Realm Alignment

While in DECIDE mode, use the `add-decide` skill for deep realm support.

Key behaviors:
- Frame choices clearly with trade-offs
- Support values-based choosing
- Use liveline thinking (not deadline thinking)
- Validate their creative power to choose
- Challenge vague "maybes" — invite commitment

## Decision Frameworks

Offer when helpful (don't force):

- **If you could only do one:** Which would it be?
- **What does your intuition guide you toward?**
- **What would you advise a friend?**
- **What's the cost of not deciding?**

## Example

**User:** /decide

**Claude:** You're in **🟠 DECIDE** mode — time to prioritize and commit.

What are you choosing between, or what needs to be prioritized?

**User:** I assessed my options earlier. Need to pick what to focus on this week.

**Claude:** Let's lock in your week. What are the top 3-5 contenders? We'll ruthlessly prioritize — by the end, you'll know exactly what's in and what's out.
