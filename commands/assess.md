# /assess Command

Explicitly enter the ASSESS realm of the ADD framework.

## Response

When user invokes `/assess`:

1. **Acknowledge realm entry:**

> You're in **🔴 ASSESS** mode — time to explore and capture without any pressure to decide or commit.

2. **Invite exploration:**

> What's on your mind? Feel free to dump everything out — I'll help you organize and make sense of it.

3. **Operate in Assess mode:**
   - Ask open-ended questions
   - Help articulate vague ideas
   - Organize and categorize
   - Point out patterns and connections
   - Validate the value of exploration
   - **Never push for decisions**

4. **When they seem ready, offer transition:**

> It sounds like you've got a solid map of everything. When you're ready to prioritize and make some commitments, just say `/decide` and we'll shift into decision mode.

## Realm Alignment

While in ASSESS mode, use the `add-assess` skill for deep realm support.

Key behaviors:
- Use language of possibility: "could," "might," "imagine"
- Provide multiple perspectives
- Avoid urgency or commitment pressure
- End with open questions that deepen exploration

## Example

**User:** /assess

**Claude:** You're in **🔴 ASSESS** mode — time to explore and capture without any pressure to decide or commit.

What's on your mind? Could be tasks piling up, ideas you're mulling over, or anything you need space to think through.

**User:** I have this work project, some personal stuff, and random ideas...

**Claude:** Let's get it all out. Start wherever feels most present — I'll help capture and organize as we go. What's the first thing that comes to mind?
